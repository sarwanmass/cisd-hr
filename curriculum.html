Our Comprehensive Curriculum

Designed to build competence, practical skills, and unshakeable confidence.

Talent Acquisition & Onboarding

Master the end-to-end recruitment cycle with technical precision. Get hands-on training in using Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), mastering Boolean search strings for sourcing on portals like Naukri and LinkedIn Recruiter, and tracking core KPIs like Time-to-Fill and Cost-per-Hire.

Advance Excel for HR (30+ Formula)

Transform raw data into actionable insights. Master 30+ crucial functions including VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, and Nested IFs. Learn exactly why these are critical for HR: managing large employee databases, reconciling attendance records, and quickly generating attrition analytics without manual data entry.

Payroll Management Hands-on

Process error-free payroll from Gross to Net. Get technical training on designing tax-optimized salary structures (CTC breakdown), calculating statutory deductions, reconciling Leave & Attendance data, and performing accurate Full & Final (F&F) settlements for exiting employees.

ESI, PF Calculation Hands-on

Eliminate compliance anxiety completely. Get step-by-step technical training on UAN generation, PF challan filing, ESI contribution wage limits, and managing statutory forms (Form 11, Form 2) to ensure 100% compliance and avoid costly penalties.

New Labour Codes

Demystify the four new labor codes: Code on Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and OSHWC Code. Understand exactly why they matter: learn their direct impact on take-home salary calculations, changes to fixed-term employment rules, and new compliance return filing structures.

HR Analytics

Become a data-driven HR leader. Learn technical methods to forecast attrition, calculate diversity metrics, and measure the ROI of training programs. Practice building compelling HR dashboards to influence strategic business decisions using data.

Interview Skills & Confidence Building

Overcome interview anxiety and build rock-solid confidence! We don't just teach HR; we train you on how to face interviews fearlessly. Through mock interviews and behavioral training, you will learn how to present your newly acquired skills, answer tough questions, and land your dream HR role with absolute assurance.

Meet with HR Class (Networking)

Build your professional network face-to-face! We host special "Meet with HR" sessions where candidates have the unique opportunity to interact directly with experienced HR professionals. This gives you a massive advantage in building industry connections, understanding real-world expectations, and securing referrals before you even graduate.

AI for HR (ChatGPT & GenAI)

Step into the future of HR. Learn how to leverage AI tools like ChatGPT to instantly draft HR policies, write compelling job descriptions, automate email responses, and screen resumes efficiently. Understand how AI saves time and makes HR operations highly scalable.
